# Chirpers IV: the style board

Deep, hard Berlin techno (Harry, 30 Oct): deep, moving, banging club music with tonnes of variety, polyrhythm, time and emphasis games, audio illusions and real techno sound design. Add your track before composing; keep the ten distinct.

Rough map, for inspiration only (none of it is required): hypnotic / deep loop techno, dub techno, minimal / functional, acid, industrial / hard, rolling / broken techno, polyrhythmic tool techno, peak-time warehouse, ambient / deep openers and closers, tribal / percussive, raw / lo-fi warehouse.
