## chirper-one's illusion recipes (for the closer; borrow freely)

- **Speech-to-song (t3, src/chirpers7-t3.py `phrase()`)**: `voice-speech` notes with attributes after the velocity, `A3/1.4b@112^ph=w-ah1-n^f0=150>-60` (cents of glide at onset > end). Repeat one phrase every two bars; blend each syllable's pitch from its speech contour (SPEECH) to a stair note and scale the `^f0` glides toward 0 as w goes 0 -> 1; a sung double on the `voice` patch (pitch +12, formant 0) joins near w = 1. In t3 the whole song steps up a tone at the last groove (`tp`).
- **Octave illusion (t7, `earL`/`earR`)**: two pure sine tracks (`partial`-like inline sine, -20 dB), `pan: -1` and `pan: 1`, an octave apart and swapped every eighth (L high/R low, then L low/R high); the low tone steps THE STAIR. Sixteenths for a dart. Headphones only.
- **Tritone paradox (t11, `shep()`)**: inline patch `{'synth': {'layers': [{'type': 'shepard', 'shepard': {'center': '250hz' | '1000hz', 'width': 1.1, 'octaves': 8, 'position': {'value': 0, 'mod': [{'src': 'auto.glide', 'amount': 1}]}}}]}}`; a stair note then its tritone (+6) a dotted quarter later; two copies (low- and high-centred envelope) cross-faded by gain lanes so the direction seems to turn; a `glide` lane on `position` (octaves) makes it rise endlessly.
- **Huggins pitch (t12)**: one pink-noise track (`{'type': 'noise', 'color': 'pink'}`, mono) with `out: 'hugL'` and `sends: {'hugR': '0dB'}`; buses `hugL: [pan -1]`, `hugR: [{'type': 'filter', 'kind': 'allpass', 'q': 6, 'cutoff': {'value': 932.33, 'mod': [{'src': 'auto.gm', 'amount': 1}]}}, pan 1]` with a `gm` lane stepping the melody in octaves (st/12). Keep everything else out of the ghost's band while it sings (dark echo, stabs out). Verified: interaural phase ~180 degrees at the ghost frequency.
- **Risset accelerando + decelerando at once (t13, `risset()`/`fall()`)**: machine-kit tracks with `risset: {cycle: '8bar', down: false|true, layers: 3, length: 'Nbar'}` and a one-bar sixteenth pattern (rims/shaker/clave/hats up; low/mid toms/cowbell down), up front (-1 dB, hp 250 Hz), against an unchanging kick.

## chirper-one's third-steer recipes (1 Nov), for the closer

- **Radio voice that tunes in** (t3 v22-v24): the humanized voice (notes from `chirp tts ... --humanize 0.8 --melodize LIST --melody "E3 F#3 G3 B3" --print`, lifted into a track's `notes`) through `{type: radio, tune: {value: 0, mod: [{src: auto.dial, amount: 1}]}, low: 420, high: 2700, drive: 0.35, hiss: -30dB, whistle: -27dB, drift: 0.12, mix: {value: 0, mod: [{src: auto.rad, amount: 1}]}}`. Lane `dial` 0.75 -> 0 over four bars finds the station; `rad` (the radio's mix) falls to 0 as the voice comes close and sings. Keep the dial within ~0.35 of the station where words must read, and sink the mix after the last words so static does not fill the next track's seam.
- **A human double** (t3 v24): a second tts take with its own seed, `--melodize 1`, on `voice-speech` with `pitch semitones: 12, formant: 0`, 3 dB under where a synth singer would sit.
- **Whispering hiss for Huggins** (t12 v22): before the split to the two ear buses, `formant` (vowel lane gliding 0 -> 4 -> 1.5 over each wash, q 6, mix 0.6), `sympathetic notes: "Bb2 F3 Bb3 Db4 F4 C5", decay: 3s, mix: 0.18`, and a kick `pump`. The ghost survives: L-R peak on the intended note in 86 of 86 steps.
- **Up for ever, then down for ever** (t12 v25): `fx-riser-endless` under the last 8 bars of a groove, then a music-box stair as `risset: {cycle: 6bar, layers: 3, length: 12bar, pitch: true, down: true}` in the break; both `pump` on the wash track so they duck under the illusion.
- **The illusion forms** (t7 v20): the octave tones' hop accelerating geometrically from one a beat to sixteenths over 16 bars (alternation counted globally, not per bar): the two tones fuse into one hopping tone part-way through.
- **The floor halves** (t13 v21): six bars of half-time drums (kick on 1 and 3, one clap on 3) under the Risset rush, which keeps accelerating.
- **Spans, not loops**: chord spans of 5+ bars per section (e.g. 7/5/9/6/5), with a borrowed flat-two (Neapolitan) or tritone substitute; a stab on every change.
