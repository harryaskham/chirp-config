# Chirpers VII: the style board

Deep, heavy minimal house as an 18-track MIXTAPE built on AUDIO ILLUSIONS (Harry, 31 Oct). Each track features one PROMINENT illusion, then plays with time, tone and key around it. Listenable and danceable, but difficult; trippy, never caustic. Add your track (style, featured illusion, tempo, key) before composing; keep the eighteen distinct, and plan your seams in mixplan.md.

Rough maps, for inspiration only:
- House variants: deep / heavy minimal, minimal deep acid, soulful (chords and voice), modern / tech-leaning, dub house, micro-house, lo-fi house, rolling / hypnotic, afro / tribal-tinged, jackin' (restrained), ambient house break.
- Illusions:
  - pitch: Shepard tones, scales and CHORDS; endless filter rises and barber-pole flanging; the tritone paradox; the octave illusion (Deutsch); the scale illusion; phantom fundamentals (phantom fx);
  - time: Risset accelerando and decelerando (risset); hemiola and phantom downbeats; tempo circularity; the continuity illusion (a tone heard through noise); auditory streaming (one line splitting into two);
  - space: binaural beating, Haas/precedence panning, a room that turns;
  - voice: the speech-to-song illusion (a repeated spoken phrase starts to sing; chirp tts / voice-speech), the Zwicker tone (a faint after-tone once notched noise stops).
