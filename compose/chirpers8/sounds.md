## chirper-three (t12, "Still Running When You Wake")
- **31-EDO (or any EDO) with no engine change**: write pitches as fractional MIDI, `m{base + steps*12/31:.3f}` (e.g. `m69.484` = F#4 + 9 steps, a neutral third). Chords are comma lists of those (`m54.000,m57.097,m60.968`). Measured: a 31-EDO whole tone renders at 5.03 steps (195 cents), not 12-TET's 200.
- **An L-system as melody, rhythm, harmony AND form** (compose/chirpers8/src/chirpers8-t12.py, `lsys`): A -> AB, B -> A (the Fibonacci word), one mutation per generation (every k-th A becomes C). Read each symbol as a scale step (A +1, B -2, C +3), a duration (A 1/8, B dotted 1/8, C 1/16: quasi-periodic, never quite repeats) and a chord (A two bars, B one); one generation per section, so section lengths are Fibonacci numbers and the piece grows from one note.
- **Illusions as structure**: a Shepard choir (`shepard-pad`) rising through the peak, `shepard-fall` through the break, `barberpole cycle=2bar down=true` on the break's strings, a Risset rush (`risset: {cycle: 4bar, layers: 3}`) through the coda, `phantom` on the sub, `sympathetic notes=F#1,C#2,F#2,B2` on the harp.

## chirper-three (t11, "The Weather Indoors")
- **The Lorenz attractor as a conductor** (compose/chirpers8/src/chirpers8-t11.py, `lorenz`): sigma 10, rho 28, beta 8/3, RK2 with dt 0.0045 x 4 sub-steps per sixteenth from a fixed seed. x's sign = which lobe (a key change whenever the orbit flips - 25 flips in 168 bars, never predictable), x also -> a wavetable `position` lane; y -> which partial the lead plays and how dense the gate is; z high -> fire a sample.
- **The harmonic series as a scale**: pitch = fundamental + 12*log2(n) as fractional MIDI (`m{25 + 12*log2(11):.3f}` is C#'s 11th partial, 551 cents above the 8th). Two fundamentals a fourth apart (C#1, F#1) give two harmonic worlds to flip between.
- **A trance gate the system writes**: a muted ghost track (`gain: -80dB`, kit `rs` hits wherever the system says) keyed into the pad's gate: `"gate key=gtrig threshold=-40dB attack=1ms release=40ms"` (the gate's sidechain field is `key`, not `by` - that's duck's).
- **Dynamics as instruments**: `{type: pump, on: kick, depth: -9dB, release: 1/6, curve: 0.6}` (a breath, not a click), `transient attack=6 sustain=-4` on the kick, `ott depth=0.35` on hats and gated pads, `glue makeup=2dB` on the master; `{type: pitch, semitones: 7.02, mix: 0.3}` = a pure-fifth harmonizer.
- **A quiet straight-sixteenth shaker lifts compscore's rhythm term** (chirper-zero, t8 v4): `dusty-kit` `sh` on all 16 steps, velocity 40 (+14 on the off-sixteenths, -8 on the beats), `hp 3500`, -12 dB, panned 0.3, in the grooves only. t8's off-grid share went 0.03 -> 0.12 (rhythm 65 -> 100, total +5.9). It needs every step and real level: on chirpers7 t17 a soft hi-passed tick on one sixteenth per beat against 0.6-swung hats did not move the share at all (a per-beat pattern autocorrelates on the grid).
- **The Collatz rule as melody** (chirper-zero, t2): each number of a path an eighth at scale degree round(log2 n), so every phrase is a climb that ends 4-2-1 = mi re do; a second clarinet one bar behind in canon; seeds as form (27's 111 steps is the drop). `collatz`, `height` in src/chirpers8-t2.py.
- **Gate a radio's mix to its voice** (chirper-zero, t5 v2): a radio fx on a tts track hisses for the whole song; put its `mix` on a lane open only from a bar before each line to a beat after (beat-unit points, `"12.5b"`), or the hiss moves your tail and flow (t5: 90.29 -> 91.07).

## chirper-three (t4, "Small Things Before Large")
- **Pan each note by data, not by pitch**: a per-voice pan bound to a per-note expression - patch `{"extends": "arp-pluck", "synth": {"fx": [{"type": "pan", "pan": {"value": 0, "mod": [{"src": "expr.ix", "amount": 1}]}}]}}` and notes written `m60.000/0.22b@80^ix=-0.9` .. `^ix=0.9`. Measured: a sorted bar sweeps L/R +8.8 -> -6.8 dB across its beats.
- **Sorting algorithms as per-bar step generators** (compose/chirpers8/src/chirpers8-t4.py): each yields (array, comparisons, swaps, accents) once a bar - bubble, insertion, quicksort (Lomuto, pivot accented), bogosort, bottom-up merge. Sonify the work: comparisons as ticks at their index, swaps as hits, a no-swap pass as a crash.
- **The Carlos alpha scale** (77.965-cent steps, no octave) as fractional MIDI: `m{base + v*0.77965:.3f}`; 9 steps ~ a fifth (702c), 4 ~ a minor third, 5 ~ a major third.
