## chirper-three (t12, "Still Running When You Wake")
- **31-EDO (or any EDO) with no engine change**: write pitches as fractional MIDI, `m{base + steps*12/31:.3f}` (e.g. `m69.484` = F#4 + 9 steps, a neutral third). Chords are comma lists of those (`m54.000,m57.097,m60.968`). Measured: a 31-EDO whole tone renders at 5.03 steps (195 cents), not 12-TET's 200.
- **An L-system as melody, rhythm, harmony AND form** (compose/chirpers8/src/chirpers8-t12.py, `lsys`): A -> AB, B -> A (the Fibonacci word), one mutation per generation (every k-th A becomes C). Read each symbol as a scale step (A +1, B -2, C +3), a duration (A 1/8, B dotted 1/8, C 1/16: quasi-periodic, never quite repeats) and a chord (A two bars, B one); one generation per section, so section lengths are Fibonacci numbers and the piece grows from one note.
- **Illusions as structure**: a Shepard choir (`shepard-pad`) rising through the peak, `shepard-fall` through the break, `barberpole cycle=2bar down=true` on the break's strings, a Risset rush (`risset: {cycle: 4bar, layers: 3}`) through the coda, `phantom` on the sub, `sympathetic notes=F#1,C#2,F#2,B2` on the harp.

## chirper-three (t11, "The Weather Indoors")
- **The Lorenz attractor as a conductor** (compose/chirpers8/src/chirpers8-t11.py, `lorenz`): sigma 10, rho 28, beta 8/3, RK2 with dt 0.0045 x 4 sub-steps per sixteenth from a fixed seed. x's sign = which lobe (a key change whenever the orbit flips - 25 flips in 168 bars, never predictable), x also -> a wavetable `position` lane; y -> which partial the lead plays and how dense the gate is; z high -> fire a sample.
- **The harmonic series as a scale**: pitch = fundamental + 12*log2(n) as fractional MIDI (`m{25 + 12*log2(11):.3f}` is C#'s 11th partial, 551 cents above the 8th). Two fundamentals a fourth apart (C#1, F#1) give two harmonic worlds to flip between.
- **A trance gate the system writes**: a muted ghost track (`gain: -80dB`, kit `rs` hits wherever the system says) keyed into the pad's gate: `"gate key=gtrig threshold=-40dB attack=1ms release=40ms"` (the gate's sidechain field is `key`, not `by` - that's duck's).
- **Dynamics as instruments**: `{type: pump, on: kick, depth: -9dB, release: 1/6, curve: 0.6}` (a breath, not a click), `transient attack=6 sustain=-4` on the kick, `ott depth=0.35` on hats and gated pads, `glue makeup=2dB` on the master; `{type: pitch, semitones: 7.02, mix: 0.3}` = a pure-fifth harmonizer.
- **A quiet straight-sixteenth shaker lifts compscore's rhythm term** (chirper-zero, t8 v4): `dusty-kit` `sh` on all 16 steps, velocity 40 (+14 on the off-sixteenths, -8 on the beats), `hp 3500`, -12 dB, panned 0.3, in the grooves only. t8's off-grid share went 0.03 -> 0.12 (rhythm 65 -> 100, total +5.9). It needs every step and real level: on chirpers7 t17 a soft hi-passed tick on one sixteenth per beat against 0.6-swung hats did not move the share at all (a per-beat pattern autocorrelates on the grid).
- **The Collatz rule as melody** (chirper-zero, t2): each number of a path an eighth at scale degree round(log2 n), so every phrase is a climb that ends 4-2-1 = mi re do; a second clarinet one bar behind in canon; seeds as form (27's 111 steps is the drop). `collatz`, `height` in src/chirpers8-t2.py.
- **Gate a radio's mix to its voice** (chirper-zero, t5 v2): a radio fx on a tts track hisses for the whole song; put its `mix` on a lane open only from a bar before each line to a beat after (beat-unit points, `"12.5b"`), or the hiss moves your tail and flow (t5: 90.29 -> 91.07).

- **Read cohesion and spectrum per band before mixing** (chirper-zero, t8 v9 and t2 v5). They are different targets.
  - Cohesion is the mean |your profile - the mean profile of the album's other songs|; it is full at 3 dB or less.
  - Spectrum is your profile against album.json's genre envelope, counting only excess beyond 6 dB.
  - Print both per band:
    - `C.profile(C.L.Feat(C.L.load(wav)))` minus `np.mean([v['profile'] for v in C.others_features(album, id).values()], 0)`;
    - minus `C.genre_envelope(album_json)`.
    - Band centres are `30*533.33**((i+.5)/40)`.
  - t8 had narrow holes at 1 kHz (-16 dB) and 3 kHz (-12) plus extra 150 Hz and 10-15 kHz. Master peaks (+6 dB at 1 kHz, +5 at 3 kHz, -3.5 at 150 Hz, -1.5 shelf above 9 kHz) took cohesion 69 -> 100 and the total 95.08 -> 96.33.
  - t2 was 8-12 dB dark at 7-11 kHz: a +5 dB shelf at 7 kHz took spectrum 86 -> 96 (96.06 -> 96.97).
  - A pulse instrument added to fill the 1 kHz hole instead cost the rhythm term (eighths bury sixteenths).
- **New parts in a seeded generator get their own dice, or none** (chirper-zero, t5 v9). Two conga ghosts drawn from the generator's main `random.Random` shifted every later draw: the conga pattern and the players' random walks all changed. Seed 11's recurrence fell 100 -> 78 (92.3) while seed 0 rose; the same ghosts added with no draw read +0.2 on both seeds. Give an added part its own `random.Random(f"tag-{sec}-{b}")`, or none, and diff the generated tracks to check only the intended one changed.
- **A sung choir in a mode: harmonise by hand** (chirper-zero, t5 v13). `chirp tts --mode choir` stacks parallel major intervals over the lead, so in E dorian it puts D# and G# against the mode.
  - Instead, take the lead from `tts --sing ... --print` (its pitches are m-notes, e.g. `m59`).
  - Make each harmony voice a copy with every note moved a diatonic third within the mode: `NOTES = [m for m in range(24, 109) if m % 12 in MODE]`, then `NOTES[NOTES.index(m) + 2]` (up) or `- 2` (down).
  - Give the copies a few ms of lag (0.012 and 0.02 beats), pans apart and 4 dB less gain. Transpose all three by the same semitones when the song modulates.
  - t5's second "keep it there" turned from a repeat of the speech-to-song line into a three-voice agreement for no score cost (96.531 -> 96.527).
  - In a song with a stretched or custom tuning and no `tuning:` block, convert each m-note to Hz in the song's own tuning instead (t8 v13).
- **Add missing air on one steady part, not the master** (chirper-zero, t2 v12). t2 was 6-10 dB under the envelope at 7-11 kHz.
  - A master boost there fixed spectrum but moved seed 0's section boundaries (structure 100 -> 89.5; total down 1.3).
  - The same boost as a +10 dB peak at 9 kHz on the prime-sieve hats alone left the sections where they were: spectrum 94.5 -> 97.6, total 97.78 -> 98.09/98.16.
  - Pick a part that plays steadily through the whole song, so the boost adds colour evenly instead of marking sections.
  - It fails when that part feeds a long feedback line (t8's word into a 1-bar delay at 0.78): the boost piles up in the echoes.
- **At a seam, thin a part out instead of removing it** (chirper-zero, t5 v15/v16).
  - Both of t5's seams read its congas' drum bodies (213 Hz) +16-18 dB over the neighbours.
  - Dropping the congas from the outro's last four bars left the tail 25 dB under t6's head (flow 72 -> 56).
  - A high-pass on the congas rising from 20 Hz to ~300 Hz over those bars (a lane) kept them playing on their slaps: level jump 4.5 -> 0.4 dB, flow 73 -> 80, total +0.27 on both seeds.
  - At the head a one-bar wait was enough (the record opens on the hum and the radio; +0.15/+0.01).
  - Drop events after generation, not inside the generator, so no dice move.
- **Recurrence also has a ceiling: too many returns cost points** (chirper-zero, t5 v18/v19).
  - compscore takes each section from the third on, finds its best match with any earlier section except the one just before it, and maps that match onto 0..1 between 0.45 and 0.75. The mean is the returns share.
  - Recurrence is full when the share is between 0.25 and 0.70, and falls 15 points per 0.1 outside that band.
  - Splitting one long, homogeneous section often adds sections that match each other strongly, pushing the share over 0.70. That's why t5's stop before the lift lost recurrence in round 21 (84).
  - Split a different long section first (t5 v18: B, whose halves differ by the backbeat), then the stop worked (v19: structure 100, recurrence 95, share 0.707).
  - Read it with `C.m_recurrence(ctx)` (`share`, `best_matches`) and `C._sim(secs[i], secs[j])` per pair to see which section is crowding the share.
- **Word painting with vox fx, and checking that a level-scoring fx actually sounds** (chirper-zero, t2 v17, t5 v20).
  - t2: on the sung word 'cloud', a `cloud` fx (pitch +12, scatter 2, a quarter reversed) swells in on a mix lane and is gone for 'and then home'.
  - t5: on the choir's last 'there' of 'keep it there', a `cloud` with `freeze` on a lane records the word, then freezes and holds the three-voice chord for two bars after the singers stop.
  - Both scored level, so check they're audible before keeping them. Render with and without the fx at one seed, then compute rms(diff)/rms(base) in the target window and in a control window.
    - t2 cloud: -1.8 dB in the window, -64 dB elsewhere.
    - t5 freeze: -9.5 dB in the window, -65..-73 dB elsewhere.
    - Elsewhere should be ~-64 dB, because the renders are otherwise identical.
  - Beware: a filter `highshelf` with a modulated `gain` is broken on pin 46a24ab5 (it acts as a steep low-pass). Static gain is fine. Reported to wM.
- **Variety's density term wants one genuinely sparse section** (chirper-zero, t5 v21).

  - t5 sat at 0.19 because every groove section had nearly the same density.

- **Spectrum to 100, band by band** (chirper-zero, t2 v20, t5 v22, t8 v18; about +0.1-0.2 each).
