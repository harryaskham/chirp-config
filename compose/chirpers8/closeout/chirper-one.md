# chirper-one: close-out (t1 Echolalia, t7 Annealing, t10 Call and Return)

**Engine.** Nothing blocks me. pvoc (641d3842) came too late. I had meant it for t1's machine voice (robot/whisper doubling) and a machine choir in t10, but I won't start that now. The engine's per-voice effects can't be reached on kit pieces, so t7's "atoms settle" scatter covers only the pitched voices, not the drums. Hz pitches are never retuned by a track tuning; that's expected, but it caught me twice.

**Patches/library.** Never landed: `kuramoto-flies` as one of t7's search colours, and `risset-bell` for t10's toll. Variety: my t1 comping briefly made `ks-harp` a staple, so I moved t1 to a Wurlitzer. t10's machine-kit kick and rimshot are still shared with t11 and t12.

**Songs.**
- **t1 Echolalia (v50, 99.46).** The plan landed: the machine learns the line and says it back, and v50 puts it in the band's room at the end. What's left: the middle of the second groove still nearly repeats its first half.
- **t7 Annealing (v46, 98.20).** It still has the album's flattest dynamics (2.5 dB). The energy lane after the compressor (v18 quench, cooling sections, coda at −7) exists, but the limiter and loudness normalisation flatten it. The cooling should be audible as level. The coda is static.
- **t10 Call and Return (v55, 97.91).** The seam from t9 never got solved. t10 opens with a bell toll at D4, and the 292 Hz band jumps about 30 dB at the join (flow ~67). Two attempts on the bell (softer, then an octave up) both made the in-join's level jump worse. The final calls' last stretch (w9) is static.

**Structure/album.**

- **Off limits:** t10's sung duet, calls2 bars 0-7, which Harry singled out, stays untouched.
- **Overused:** voice-speech is in 8 songs.
