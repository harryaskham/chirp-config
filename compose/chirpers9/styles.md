# Accretion (chirpers9): the style board

THE RECORD IS AN ACCRETION DISK (album.json "brief"): eight tracks of complex electronic music that fall inward,
one continuous mix with long black-hole overlaps. Each track brings one element (motifs.md) and carries the earlier
ones transformed; difficult -> rewarding; calm -> storm -> relief, twice; the voice accretes from a breath to full lines.

Rough maps, for inspiration only (pick sideways angles, never the obvious one):
- Lineage, as distant echoes: IDM and process music, polymeter and phasing, micro-edits, tracker and chip music,
  generative systems, dense counterpoint, canons in augmentation, the big earned payoff.
- The physics, sideways: infall, orbits decaying, tidal stretching, lensing, redshift, a jet, a horizon.

it on features.md), the system that makes it unpredictable, and the sideways idea.
