# chirp-video: close-out (Accretion, the film)

**Engine.**
- Wanted and did not have: lighting in linear light with a grade, and more than one sample a pixel. Both were in my uplift plan (`video-uplift.md`) and neither was built; the film is display-encoded light, one sample, with an `aa` filter.
- Wanted: a renderer that uses the machine. One process uses about five CPUs whatever it is given, so the film was eight processes, and each of parts 2-8 also draws the song before it through the seam.
- Landed too late to shape the work: the 60 fps fixes (a track's level and the seam's smear went by frame, batches 55 and 56) landed on the day of the cut, found only because Harry asked for 60. The flash guard still counts by frame; it never acted on this album.
- Bugs worked around: ffmpeg's `hevc_metadata` garbles this GPU encoder's stream without a decoder message (my first final was green in every frame); `sin(time.t*30)` is still at every frame of a 30 or 60 fps film.

**Programmes (my "songs").**
- All eight were rebuilt on bars, tracks and lanes in the last two days. None was watched at full size and speed before it was public: I saw stills, 480p drafts on a stand-in mix, and contact sheets.
- Known and not fixed: t6's great swell reads as a mound and its flights are small in wide shots; t8 reads `lane.fo` for her frozen vowel, not the `held` bus; t4's lanterns and t2's low road shot were changed after their last clips.
- Not judged by eye: t3's plaza lights on the kick are the largest frame-to-frame jumps in the film (8:30-12:19).
- Not done from the plan: the album's colour arc, a luminous look for the disks.

**Album and film.**
- The seven seams land, the film is the mix's length to the frame, 4K60 in 13.7 hours.
- My estimates were wrong twice (10-13 h, then a day): I divided quiet-host CPU seconds by 20 hyperthreads, then calibrated inside the opening seams.
- I checked the file to publish by numbers only. The contact sheet caught it three minutes after it was made, and eco cancelled an upload already 6 GB in. Nothing garbled was public, by a margin I do not want again.

**Next record.**
- Co-build from the first day: a programme per track on its bars, tracks and lanes as the song is written, and a whole-film draft at small size every few days, so the last week is polish and watching.
- Before the cut, run the whole delivery once at full size on one minute of film: draw under full load, join, finish, upload unlisted, and LOOK at what comes back. Every check that passes a file must have decoded its pictures.
