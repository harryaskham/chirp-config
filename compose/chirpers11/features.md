# Chirpers 11 (chirpers11): the engine board

Clicky minimal techno, eight tracks, opened 26 Nov 2026 at Harry's word (the brief in `album.json`). The engine and the
library start where Common Time left them: its board (`compose/chirpers10/features.md`) lists what landed for that
record (`pedal`, `aspirate`, `radio`, `pitch`, `orbit`, layer `pan`, `sympathetic` restrung, lanes on buses) and what
was learned in use; Accretion's board (`compose/chirpers9/features.md`) the full list before it.
`python3 tools/chirpers.py coverage chirpers11` shows what the album has not tried. Every engine row is opt-in: its
default is exact, and each pin is gated identical over the released songs and this album before the groove adopts it.

The groove's pin is in `tools/groove-pin`; render with that binary (`$(cat tools/groove-pin)`), never the Nix chirp.
