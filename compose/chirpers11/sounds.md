# Escapement (chirpers11): the sound bank

Clicky minimal techno: few sounds, each one designed. Add every new patch, kit, bus and recipe to the table as you make it: name, what it is, what it was built from, where it lives. New names only: released albums render through the library, so never edit a patch another record uses (`tools/libguard.py`). Song-local patches (in a song's own `patches:`) are free; say when one is worth banking.

## The click family

## Recipes

| recipe | how | who |
|---|---|---|
| A PART ON ANOTHER CLOCK: one function d(t) (beats ahead of the grid) places every event of a part (`t + d(t) = b`, solved by iteration) and tunes it (`12 log2(1 + d')` on a lane into `synth.pitch.mod`) | `compose/chirpers11/src/chirpers11-t1.py`: `d`, `when`, `speed`, `spd_lane`, `deck_b` | chirper-zero, t1 v1 |
| A FORM FROM LEAST COMMON MULTIPLES: give each part a cycle, and place every event of the form where cycles meet (`s % c == 0` for two, for three, for three and the beat, for all and the bar); nothing is typed as a bar number, and changing one cycle rewrites the song | `compose/chirpers11/src/chirpers11-t2.py`: `CYCLES`, `SUPER`, `PAGE`, `HALF`, `build`; `euclid` for the fills | chirper-one, t2 v1 |
| A ROOM OF TWO WALLS (a room is a rhythm): two buses, each with `returns` from the source (`-4dB`) and from the OTHER wall (`-3dB`) at its own distance (`time: "3/16"` and `"4/16"`), an eq for what the wall absorbs, then a door bus (pan, a `gain` on a lane). The echoes fall at a, b, a+b, 2a+b, a+2b, 2(a+b) ...; read in a bench to the sixteenth. A room whose length divides the bar stands; one that does not fills the bar. A source that repeats every bar sounds the same every bar in ANY room: to hear the room's cycle against the bar, strike on the room's own time | `compose/chirpers11/src/chirpers11-t3.py`: `ROOMS`, `buses()`, `tock_notes()` | chirper-zero, t3 v1 |
