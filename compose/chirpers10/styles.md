# Common Time (chirpers10): the style board

One track for now: "Common Time", about thirteen minutes, chirper-zero's (album.json "brief" has Harry's words in full).

What Harry asked for, in short:
- a single, super rich djent / Meshuggah-style epic: different sections and styles, a motif and rhythm that grows and gets splayed, as in their longest tracks;
- long cycles of polyrhythm that line up after a long wait, taken sideways (time crystals and synchronicity were his examples; nothing on the nose, no numbers in titles);
- the name is the irony: common time is 4/4, and the piece will use mad time signatures;
- the sound first: a new family of patches built on the old ones with today's engine (sounds.md);
- room to experiment: every engine patch, segment and idea can be tried and finessed inside it.

The board, as on earlier records: claim the row, and keep it true as the piece changes.
