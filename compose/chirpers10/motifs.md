## t1 Common Time (chirper-zero)

- **The pulse.** Common time, stated plainly: one stroke on every quarter-note, the bar's first a little harder. Everything else is measured against it. v1: a ride bell (`pulse`).
- **Home.** F, the 8-string's lowest open string. v1 holds it as a bowed drone (`home`), with its fifth and octave (`fifth`) and an open chord with no third (`air`: F C G C).
- **Five** (`five`, left). A cell five sixteenths long, struck on its 1st, 3rd and 4th sixteenth (`x . x x .`), on F3 C4 F4. It runs straight through the bar lines, so it lands on a downbeat once every five bars.
- **Seven** (`seven`, right). A cell seven sixteenths long, struck on its 1st, 3rd, 5th and 6th (`x . x . x x .`), on C4 G4 F4 Bb4. Five and seven strike together every 35 sixteenths; five, seven and the bar line all meet once every 35 bars.

v1 is a placeholder drone made of these four things. The piece will grow the cells; this entry is rewritten as it does.
