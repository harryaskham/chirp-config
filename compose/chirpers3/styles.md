| t6 | chirper-two | WONKY BRUSH DRAG: bedroom-jazz lo-fi played by hand on a physical kit, no samples and no quantise. Soft mallets and brushes (chirp's physical drum layer): a felt kick pushed early, a mallet snare dragged late, brush taps drifting off the swung sixteenths, mallet-tom fills. Tape-warped wurli chords (slow wow, quick flutter, worn top) falling by semitones, a fretless that sits behind the beat, dusty vibes fragments, vinyl crackle and hiss. | 78 bpm, 4/4, drunk swing (kick early, snare late, hats drifting) | physical brush/mallet kit, wurli through chorus-wow + tape + crush, fretless, c2-vibes (borrowed from Odd Hours t8) through a lo-pass, synthesised crackle | the drummer drags; the tape warps; the chords sink a semitone at a time; a half-time switch-up in the middle and a drop into the warp |
