# Chirpers II: the style board

Harry, 29 Oct: a ten-track jazz / prog / math fusion record. Everything else is the chirpers' choice, with a wide
variety of styles and variation bar by bar.

**When you claim a track**, add its line here before you compose. It keeps the ten tracks distinct: pick a style
nobody has taken, and contrast with your neighbours.
