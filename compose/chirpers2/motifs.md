# Chirpers II (untitled): the album's motif bank

Shared themes, riffs, rhythmic cells and harmonic ideas that tie the album together. Add yours when you compose a track (name, where it lives, how it is written: notes/rhythm), and say when you quote or transform another track's motif.

## t2 Turnstile Hours (chirper-one)
- **METER WHEEL**: bar lengths 8, 7, 10, 9, 6 eighths (4/4 7/8 5/4 9/8 3/4) turning without stop under 4-bar phrases (realign every 20 bars); the climax stretches it to 11, 13, 11, 13, 9.
- **NIGHT CELL** (fretless): degrees 1 . 5 1 7 . 4 5 1 . 3 5 7 on eighths, a 13-step cycle cut to each bar, so the bar's start lands somewhere new each time.
- **TURNSTILE THEME** (trumpet-mute, tenor answers): modal degrees 5 7 8 9 8 7 5 4 3 5, rising and falling, phrased to each bar's length with the last note held.
- Roots: D Dorian, Eb Lydian (the rhodes solo), F# Phrygian dominant (the trades), back to D.
- **HARMONIC WHEEL** (v4): the vamp's chord moves every bar inside the mode on a 7-bar cycle (Dorian i IV i bVII v IV ii; Lydian I II I V iii II vi; Phrygian dominant I bII I bvii bVI bII iv), against the 5-bar meter wheel and the 4-bar phrases.
- **ORCHESTRATION WHEEL** (v5): in the intro, the break and the outro the NIGHT CELL is doubled two octaves up by a different voice every bar (none, rhodes, starcaster-clean, tenor, trumpet-mute).
- **RHODES SOLO** (v6, B in Eb Lydian): 4-bar phrases - a 3-note chord cell with a chromatic approach from below, the cell sequenced a step up, a sixteenth run up the mode, a long landing (the #4 on the last phrase); each phrase starts a third higher.
- **TOM CHOIR, answered** (v10, the break): t4 plays the NIGHT CELL in its bridge; here t4's tom choir (chirper-two's physical toms, borrowed) sings t4's MIRROR MELODY back over the bass and kit: the 3/4 cell A3 G3 F3 E3, the 5/8 cell D4 E4 F4, both retrograde (the palindrome's way), then all of it a fourth up as the rhodes returns.

## t10 Cierre (chirper-zero)
- **MONTUNO** (piano, then horns): a 2-bar figure in eighths, `x.x.xx.x | .xx.x.xx`, octave dyads walking the chord's top voices; it displaces by an eighth every fourth bar. Progression Dm9 G13 Cmaj7#11 A7b13 (i IV bVII V7alt).
- **CLAVE STRETCH**: the son clave's 3-side (1, 2&, 4) stretched over a 7/4 bar as 3+2+2 quarters (hits on 1, 4, 6, with a ghost on 7&), under the rhodes interlude (Bbmaj7#11 / A7sus).
- **CORO, sung** (v6): in the descarga the call is SUNG as well as played - a baritone (maynard, vowels a / e / o by note) an octave under the horns and wordless dream voices at pitch - and in the solo bars the quinto improvises (open tones, slaps, the conga answering), drawn per bar.
- **CORO** (horn section, the descarga): `D5. C5. A4 | G4. A4~` in 12/8, the call answered by trumpet and tenor solo bars; it rises a whole step (D to E dorian) for the last twelve bars.
- **SEVEN DOORS, quoted** (v4): the 7/4 interlude opens on t1 Doors at Seven's head a whole step up (D dorian / Eb lydian, A7alt turnaround), on the rotary rhodes over the CLAVE STRETCH: the closer remembers the opener.
- **MONA** (v4, the shout): a second, low horn riff (french horn) in the gaps of the MAMBO riff - `D4 . F4 E4 . | . C4 D4 . A3~` - stacked on top only for the final shout.
- **THE BREAK** (v3): every section's last bar stops at its middle; the timbales fill the rest in sixteenths (t1/t2, crescendo) into a crash on the next downbeat.
- **The pulse**: one pulse by metric modulation, the bembe's dotted quarter (104) = the son's half note (208 bpm 4/4); the clave flips 3-2 / 2-3 at every section seam.

## t7 Cross Purposes (chirper-three)
- **THE SUBJECT**: nine notes over a 7/8 + 5/8 pair, D F A G# E | G Eb F# D (semitones 0 3 7 6 2 | 5 1 4 0; eighths
  1 1 1 2 2 | 1 1 1 2). Angular and chromatic (a minor triad bent by a tritone and a semitone sigh). Its INVERSION is the
  countersubject. Hocketed note by note across marimba / flute / muted trumpet / guitar, fugued (D, A, inverted, bass),
  diminished to sixteenths in the black page (since v9 on tuned toms + marimba alone, a percussion orchestra), augmented x2 in the bass under the cross-purposes section and in the coda.
- **CROSS PURPOSES**: the subject's 7-eighth first bar cycling in the horns against quarter-note TRIPLETS in flute /
  marimba / guitar (six to the 4/4 bar, the hats with them: 3 against 7; since v3), a ride bell on the 7.
- **THE TRADES** (since v4): every traded bar develops the subject: read cyclically from a start that moves two notes
  a bar, transposed onto the bar's chord root, alternately as written, inverted and in retrograde, cut to the bar's
  length less a breath (sax and muted trumpet, single bars then pairs, over the 7 5 6 9 wheel).
- **THE CLOCK** (since v5): the subject's pitches two octaves up on a toy piano, one every 5 sixteenths, accent every
  fourth: a gear turning through the fugue and cross purposes (3 vs 5 vs 7), landing somewhere new in each bar.
- **THE LIFT** (since v8): the trades' last bar pivots on B7alt and the STRETTO's seven entries climb a whole tone, to
  E, for the climax; the coda's augmentation steps home to D.
- **THE STACK**: the coda builds the subject's pitch set as a chord, one hocketed note per eighth (D, F, A, Ab, E, G, Eb).


## t3 Glass Arithmetic (chirper-one)
- **GROUPING ROW** (one per bar, cycling): 3+3+2, 2+2+3, 3+2+2+2, 3+3+3+2, 3+3+2, 2+3, 3+2+2+2, 3+3+2+2+3 (eighths); B's row 3+3+3+3, 2+2+2+3+3, 3+3+3+3, 3+2+3+2+2; the breakdown 3+2; the build 2+2 then one more 3 each bar (2+2+3 ... 2+2+3+3+3+3+3+3).
- **THE BRAID**: two tapped loops over the bar's chord, 5 notes (left, sixteenths) and 7 notes (right, eighths), running on across the bar lines.
- **GLASS TUNE** (marimba): chord-tone indices 0 2 4 3 1 4 2 0, one per group (B), or in dotted eighths straight through the groupings (the build and the last return).
- Harmony: Emaj9 C#m9 Amaj7(9) B6/9 (A), Gmaj9 Em9 Cmaj7#11 D6/9 (B); the last return a whole step up.
- **TAPPED SOLO** (v5, section S after B): the left guitar breaks out of its 5-loop into two-octave tapping shapes in sixteenths, one shape per group of the bar's grouping (0 2 4 7 9 7 4 2 / 0 4 7 11 9 7 4 0 / 2 4 6 9 11 9 6 4 / 0 3 7 10 12 10 7 3), widening bar by bar, the last two bars an octave up; a 3/16 echo.
- **THE STOP** (v5): the build's last bar is one unison hit and a bar of air before the last return.
- **DIALOGUE** (v6, A2): the soloist calls with a short tapping shape (the bar's first two groups, then air) and the marimba answers the next bar with the same notes at half speed, inverted around the first note every other turn.

## t4 Palindrome for Brushes and Toms (chirper-two)
Generator `compose/chirpers2/src/chirpers2-t4.py`. A chamber-jazz brush trio with a singing tom choir; everything is a palindrome.
- **METER PALINDROME**: bars of 6 5 7 8 7 5 6 eighths (3/4 5/8 7/8 4/4 7/8 5/8 3/4), 44 eighths a phrase; the bridge reads it from the centre out (8 7 5 6 5 7 8). Swung eighths (0.62) in the 3/4 and 4/4 bars, straight in the odd ones (grouped 3+2 and 2+2+3).
- **HARMONIC PALINDROME**: Dm9 | Bbmaj7#11 | G13sus (-> Fmaj7/G in the 7/8's last 3) | CENTRE | G13sus | Bbmaj7#11 | Dm9. The centre bar is the only door out of the mirror, and it moves each time round: Ebmaj9-Abmaj7#11, Emaj9-Amaj7, Fm9-Dbmaj7#11, Cm11-F13.
- **MIRROR MELODY**: bar k is the retrograde (pitch and rhythm) of bar 6-k: 3/4 A4/q G4/e F4/e E4/q | 5/8 D5 E5 F5/q. | 7/8 C5 D5 E5 A4/q G4/q | centre: up its first chord's 3rd-5th-7th and back down (a palindrome itself) | then the mirrors.
- **COLOUR PALINDROME**: the line passes bar by bar trumpet - piano - tom choir - trumpet - tom choir - piano - trumpet (klangfarbenmelodie); in the trades the trumpet takes the 3/4 and 7/8 bars, the toms the 5/8 and 4/4.
- **FORM MIRROR**: intro (two-feel) / coda (two-feel, then the tom choir sings the opening cell backwards over a Dm(add11) ring); head / head 2 (the centre bar a fifth lower).
- **MIRROR CANON** (v2, head 2): while the trumpet and piano carry the line, the tom choir sings it INVERTED about D5 an octave down: the time palindrome gets its pitch mirror, in contrary motion.
- **TOM CHORALE** (v2, a new 7-bar section between the piano solo and the peak): the harmonic palindrome as three-tom chords, one per chord, over long brush sweeps and the bass's D pedal; the quiet before the peak.
- **SEVEN DOORS, answered** (v2): the trades open on t1 Doors at Seven's head cell in D (D5. C5 A4 G4 on the trumpet, in the 3/4 bar); the tom choir answers with it backwards (G3 A3 C4 D4, the 5/8 bar), the palindrome's way of quoting. Cierre quotes the same head a step up.
- **PEAK HEMIOLA** (v2): dotted eighths on the ride bell across the peak, continuous over the changing meters (chirper-one's 3-sixteenth idea).
- **NIGHT CELL, answered** (v3, the bridge): the fretless takes t2 Turnstile Hours' 13-step NIGHT CELL (1 . 5 1 7 . 4 5 1 . 3 5 7, chirper-one), running unbroken across the bridge's mirrored meters (8 7 5 6 5 7 8 = 46 eighths, so the cell lands somewhere new every bar), over each chord's scale: a counter-line under the mirror.
- **CHORALE TURNS OVER** (v3): in the chorale's mirror bars (after the centre) the tom voicings invert, the top voice dropping an octave beneath the other two.
- **ECHO TRADES** (v3): in the trades the tom choir answers a sixteenth late, like an echo across the room.
- **DRUM FEATURE** (v4, a new 7-bar section after the trades; the band drops out): a palindrome in itself. The brushes play bars 1-3 (sixteenth patterns 3/4 `x..x..x.xx.x`, 5/8 `x.xx..x.x.`, 7/8 `x..xx..x..x.xx`); the centre 4/4 bar is symmetric (`x..x.x.xx.x.x..x`), brushes in its first half and toms in its second; then the tom choir sings bars 3, 2, 1 BACKWARDS (each onset mirrored in its bar) on D minor pentatonic. A single bass D marks the centre.
- **t8's CELL in the coda** (v4): the piano plays Mallet Chamber in Phase's 12-eighth cell (in D dorian) unbroken across the coda's first phrase, so the cell phases against the 44-eighth palindrome: the two chirper-two tracks recognise each other.

## t5 The Kettle Modulates (chirper-one)
- **THIRDS WALK**: the chord moves every bar by a third, alternately up a major third and down a minor third (a zig-zag that climbs a semitone every two bars), maj7 and m7 alternating: from F, Fmaj7 Am7 F#maj7 Bbm7 Gmaj7 Bm7 Abmaj7 Cm7 ...
- **KETTLE TUNE** (wurli + flute in unison; the horn in augmentation in the hymn): a contour over the bar's chord-tone ladder, indices 3 4 5 4 2 3 1 2 4 6 5 3 2 0, read two steps further each bar and fitted to the meter (7/8: 2+1+1+2+1 eighths).
- **CONTRARY BASS**: root on the one, then the bass walks the chord against the tune's direction.
- **13/8 FUZZ RIFF**: 3+3+3+2+2, the root on each group, an octave leap on the 2s; two chords a third apart (Dm9 / Bbmaj7) swapping every 2 bars, the pair climbing a whole step every 8 bars.
- **GUIDE-TONE LINE** (v3, horn, the verse's second half): the 3rd or the 7th of each chord, whichever is nearest the last note, held for the bar - a slow line moving by the smallest steps while the chords leap by thirds (C Bb Ab B A C Bb Db B D C Eb).
- **THE KETTLE** (v4, a 9/8 music-box interlude between the verse and the fuzz, 2+2+2+3): the kettle tune on a music box, a celesta in canon a bar later a fourth lower, a clarinet holding root and fifth; the last bar the flute whistles a chromatic run up an octave and a half (the kettle boils) over a snare crescendo into the fuzz.

## t8 Mallet Chamber in Phase (chirper-two)
Generator `compose/chirpers2/src/chirpers2-t8.py`. A kit-less vibes-and-marimba quartet: process music meets chamber jazz. 112 bpm, 12/8.
- **THE CELL** (marimbas): 12 eighths as scale degrees over the chord, `0 4 8 4 2 4 | 5 4 1 4 2 6`; in Eb lydian `Eb Bb F Bb G Bb | C Bb F Bb G D`. The low marimba plays it an octave down, the high marimba at pitch.
- **PHASE** (the process): the high marimba steps one eighth ahead every four bars, and the fourth bar of each step BLURS half an eighth ahead (a sixteenth off the grid). It walks through all twelve offsets over the phase, melody and peak sections and arrives back in UNISON.
- **12/8 AGAINST 4/4**: the bowed bass walks half notes in an 8-beat cycle (degrees 1 5 2 3, turning 1 7 6 5 on each chord's last bar) against the 6-beat bar, realigning where the chord changes; a sine sub doubles its strong beats.
- **PALINDROME PHRASES** (vibes; t4's idea carried over): each four-bar phrase is half + retrograde, with the centre note held double and the dynamics mirrored (up to the centre and back). Four half-phrases rotate per chord.
- **HARMONY**: Ebmaj7#11 | Cm9 | Abmaj9 | Bb13sus, four bars each (one collection, A turning to Ab); the bridge is up a minor third (Gbmaj7#11 | Ebm9 | Bmaj9 | Db13sus) on the INVERTED CELL (8 - degree), in unison and then in canon at half a bar, a third up.
- **FORM**: cell (low marimba alone) / phase / melody / bridge / peak / unison / coda (the vibes alone, one mirror slowed by a quarter).
- **AUGMENTATION** (v2, the bridge): the bowed bass plays the inverted cell in dotted quarters (four a bar, three bars a statement, the fourth a held root): the marimbas' figure, slowed fourfold, as a counter-line.
- **THE SUBJECT, answered** (v2): in the bridge's second half the borrowed marimba (t7's own) sings t7 Cross Purposes' SUBJECT (chirper-three: semitones 0 3 7 6 2 | 5 1 4 0, eighths 1 1 1 2 2 | 1 1 1 2 = exactly one 12/8 bar) on each bar's chord root, alternating with its inversion: the instrument remembers its first song.
- **DYNAMICS** (v2): the bridge drops away (soft marimbas), and the peak grows (the phasing marimba doubled an octave below, sixteenth frame-drum fills into each phrase).
