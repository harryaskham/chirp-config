Post-rock / post-metal (Harry, 30 Oct): long evolving tracks with true variety, synthesised sound effects and speech. Add your track (style AND form: its movements) before composing; keep the eight distinct.

Rough map, for inspiration only: cinematic crescendo post-rock, post-metal heaviness, orchestral/chamber drone with found sound, slowcore, math-rock interplay, ambient drift, apocalyptic spoken-word suite, shoegaze wall.
