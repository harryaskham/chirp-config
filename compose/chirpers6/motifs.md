## t6 The Last Train (chirper-three)
Generator `compose/chirpers6/src/chirpers6-t6.py`. Post-rock / post-metal journey, 84 bpm, E minor -> E major, ~10 min.
- **THE THEME** (clean guitar, eighth-note arpeggios, a chord a bar): Em9 `E3 B3 F#4 G4 B4 G4 F#4 B3` | Cmaj7#11 `C3 G3 E4 F#4 B4 F#4 E4 G3` | G6/B `B2 G3 D4 E4 G4 E4 D4 G3` | Dsus2 `D3 A3 E4 A4 D5 A4 E4 A3`; the MELODY over it `B4~~~ | A4~ G4~ | D5~~ B4 | A4~~~`.
- **ITS TRANSFORMATIONS**: in fragments (the tunnel: two or three arpeggio notes far apart through a long delay); ground into THE RIFF (freight: since v3 FIVE eighths, E E G F# D on an 8-string's low E, against the 4/4 (the seven-against-four is chirper-zero's Night Freight), doubled by paired chug rigs and a fuzz bass, the kick on the riff's 1 and 4), the melody AUGMENTED above it; in E MAJOR (arrival: E | C#m7 | Aadd9/C# | Bsus4-B, the melody B4 | C#5 B4 | E5 C#5 | D#5); and its first bar alone at the end.
- **THE FIELD**: rain, wind, a train passing (a brown-noise rumble swelling and panning by, rail-joint clacks speeding up then slowing, a two-tone horn A3/C#4), radio static; THE STATION VOICE (since v3 it SPEAKS, ARPAbet through `voice-speech` as in chirper-two's The Mast, through a PA band-pass): "Platform two. The last train is now departing." and at the end, slower and lower, "The last train has gone. The lights are left on."; THE RADIO MONOLOGUE in the tunnel stays a formant voice in a speech rhythm, band-passed and crushed, words just out of reach.


## t5 (chirper-one): apocalyptic spoken-word suite, "Leave the Lights On"

- **THE SIGNAL**: B4 G4 A4 E4 (falling, then answering), half-notes - glockenspiel (II), e-bow an octave up (II's second half, III), the top of the tremolo wall (III), tape-echo lead (IV), music box in E MAJOR, G -> G# (V).
- **THE WORDS**: "this is the last broadcast. if you can hear this, leave the lights on." - wM's speech voice (ARPAbet, ^f0 intonation paths), close and dry with a little tape wow, twice in I (the second breaking off); "leave the lights on" alone, closer, in V.
- **THE LETTER** (v3): over the procession the narrator reads on, a sentence every few bars with the music between - "the city is quiet now." "the buses stopped at nine." "someone left a radio on in an empty room." "and every window is lit." (ARPAbet with falling sentence-final intonation).
- **THE CRESCENDO** (III): five stages of eight bars - toms and ride, then crash, then kick and snare and the e-bow theme, then the ride's edge wash - the tremolo guitars (thirty-seconds on the chord tones) and strings louder each stage; the sub enters at stage two.
- **THE FIRE** (IV): E5 - C5 - G5 - D5 on two chug rigs hard left and right (one, and-of-2, 3, 4, and-of-4), fuzz bass and sub on the roots, half-time kit, the drums doubling in the last eight bars; the wall tape-stops over the last two beats into V.
- **THE ECHO**: the clean guitar, the tremolo and the glock feed a dotted-eighth (3/16) tape delay - the post-rock cascade against the beat.
- **THE DOOR OPENS** (v2): through III the whole mix sits behind a master low-pass that starts at 4 kHz and opens across the five stages, wide open as IV lands - the crescendo heard as distance closing; in IV the strings and the lead pump against the kick (-5 / -3 dB, 500 ms).
- **THE LOW END** (since v2, Harry's "basslines are a bit weak"): a mono 808 sub doubling the roots an octave under the fuzz bass through Departure and Arrival (pumped by the kick), the fuzz bass +5 dB, the freight's distorted bass +3, the kit +3; the train's rumble and the drone high-passed so the sub-rumble does not cloud the bass; darker on top (a high shelf from 5 kHz).

## t3 Night Freight (chirper-zero)
Generator `compose/chirpers6/src/chirpers6-t3.py`. Instrumental post-metal, 132 bpm, B minor in drop-tuned guitars (the doom movement in A); 7/8 (the crane) against 4/4 (the crescendo). Freight by sea (v2: off the rails; trains are The Last Train's). Seven movements, 8:12: QUAYSIDE, JOINTS, CHUG, STACKS, CLIMB, UNDERTOW, LAST SHIP.
- **THE JOINTS**: a gantry crane's chain clanking through its sheave - clank-clank ... clank-clank on a 14-sixteenth cycle, a synthesised port sound - and the 2+2+3 riff built on it: a clean 8-string arpeggio of seven eighths (accents on 1, 3 and 5), two shapes alternating bar by bar; from the joints' 25th bar a second guitar's natural harmonics answer it.
- **THE CHANGES**: Bm(add9) | Gmaj7(9) | D(add9) | A(add9), a chord every two 7/8 bars; in UNDERTOW the floor drops to A - A C Bb | A G | C Bb | G A, half-time doom.
- **THE CHUG IN FOUR STAGES** (v2): the riff drop-tuned (palm-muted 16ths into an open power chord); then its grouping rotated 3+2+2 (the open chord first); then THE LINE over it; then stabs on every group over double kick, and a single stab before the stacks.
- **THE LINE**: F#4 | D4 E4 | A4 | C#5 B4 - tremolo-picked over the chugs, augmented across four 4/4 bars a note on the ebow in the stacks, an octave up over the tremolo walls.
- **THE QUAYSIDE**: a buoy bell struck by the swell at irregular gaps (9, 7, 11, 6, 10, 8, 12 sixteenths), waves, a ship's diesel throbbing, the crane chain rising from nothing, the foghorn's two low tones (B and F#); in LAST SHIP the foghorn further out, the clanks fading under the clean riff.
- **THE OPERATOR** (v2): the crane operator's intercom in the stacks - "seven, clear to lift", "lowering", "hold", "seven, all clear" (the seven is the meter) - and in LAST SHIP "last one, lights stay on" (voice-speech, ARPAbet, a walkie-talkie band and drive).
- **7 AGAINST 4**: in UNDERTOW the crane's 14-sixteenth cycle comes back over the 16-sixteenth doom bars and neither gives way; then the doom thins to one held A chord every two bars with an ebow crying A-G-E over it.
- **THE INVERSION**: in the CLIMB the bass walks the riff downwards (root, b7, 5, 4) under the stretched clean riff, then the tremolo walls.
- **THE LIFT** (v3): the climb's last 16 bars, the full kit, go up a whole tone into C# - the line on the ebow with them - so the undertow's fall to A lands a major third down.
- **THE DRUMMER** (v3): tom fills (t1 t1 t2 t2 t3 t3 ft ft, rising) on the last half-bar of every 8-bar phrase in the chug, the climb and the undertow; snare rolls swelling into the chug and into the climb's full kit; reverse-cymbal swells drawn into the chug, the climb's full kit, the undertow and the last ship.
## t1 The Mast (chirper-two)
- **T, THE THEME**: F#4 E4 D4 A3 B3 (2 1 1 2 4 beats), falling and lifting; never played the same way twice: slow in delay (I), answered in eighths (II), at half speed on celesta (III), in triplets (IV), in long values a tone up on a tape-echo lead with glockenspiel (V), its first three notes on piano with no ending (VI).
- **THE NIGHT SERVICE** (radio voice, ARPAbet, a syllable a beat): "This is the night service. All the lights are left on. Keep your radio on." / "Snow on the line. We are still here." / at the end, slowed and lowered: "All the lights are left on."
- **THE VIOLIN'S ANSWER** (climb): F#5 E5 D5 C#5 B4 A4 B4 D5, the theme's shape stretched upward.
- **v2**: the climb's and the peak's tremolo in SEXTUPLETS; THE FALSE SUMMIT (the band cuts away for four bars, strings and the radio: "Keep your radio on."); THE LIGHTS FLICKER (inside the peak, eight bars of half-time kick, sub, mains hum and relay clicks, the theme slowed on a far glockenspiel); then a CHOIR sings the theme at half speed against the lead; THE BEACON (the mast's red light, a ping every two bars) in the carrier and the coda.
- Chords: brushes Dmaj9 A6 Bm9 Gmaj7#11, then Bm9 Gmaj7 D/F# Em9; snowfall Gmaj7 Em9 Bm9 A6; climb Bm G D A (G D A Bm); the lights in E: E B C#m A.


## t7 (chirper-one): math-rock interplay, "Floor 31"

- **THE FIGURE**: D F A C A F E (seven notes), tapped eighths on clean-hp; each bar takes the next N notes of the cycle, where N is that bar's eighth count (10 in 5/4, 7 in 7/8, 6 in 6/8, 8 in 4/4), so the meter re-cuts it.
- **THE INTERLOCK**: guitar 2 (clean-chorus) plays its own 4/4 eighth-note phrase of chord tones (Dm9 - Bbmaj7 - Fmaj7 - Am7 per 4 beats), ignoring the 7/8 bar lines; the two realign every seven bars (28 beats).
- **THE LIFT**: celesta chime (E6 then C6) and voice-tts announcements, band-limited 300 Hz-4.5 kHz: "going up, floor thirty one", "floor nine", "floor seventy nine", "floor fifty nine", "doors closing", "going up" (the atrium), "ground floor" (the roof's end).
- **THE MACHINE**: 7/8 drums on the 2+2+3 (kick, snare, kick-kick, snare); the chug rigs on the root and fifth at 0, 0.5, 2, 2.5, 3; the last sixteen bars in 4/4 with tremolo and strings.
- **A FLOOR UNDER IT** (v2, mix): the sub's 32-52 Hz tones were booming 10 dB over the genre while the guitars' and bass's body (100-210 Hz) sat 6-9 dB under; now a 34 Hz high-pass, -4 dB at 45 Hz and the sub 2 dB down, +5 dB at 115 Hz and +3 at 170 Hz for the body, and -3 dB at 4.2 kHz for a darker top.
